Privacy Policy

Caderninho de Receitas · Last updated: August 24, 2026 · Versão em português

Summary: your data stays on your computer. Caderninho de Receitas has no accounts, no servers of its own, and does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your information.

1. Who we are

Caderninho de Receitas is an independently published app for managing recipes, kitchen inventory, and costs. For any privacy question, contact: caderninho-de-receitas@outlook.com.

2. Data stored locally

Everything you enter in the app — recipes, ingredients, inventory, suppliers, financial records, and preferences — is stored exclusively in a local database on your device. We have no access to this data, and it never passes through servers of ours.

Backups are files created on your device, in a folder you choose. Managing, sharing, and deleting those files is entirely under your control.

3. Optional third-party services

Two optional features communicate with external services, only when you use them:

Invoice scanning (OCR). If you choose to scan an invoice, the image is sent directly from your device to the Google Gemini API, using your own API key. Invoices may contain personal data (names, tax numbers, addresses); Google's handling of that image is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. We do not use this feature on your behalf and never see what is sent.

Product lookup by barcode. When you look up a product, the barcode is queried against the public Open Food Facts database. Only the barcode is sent; as with any internet request, your IP address is visible to that service.

4. What we don't do

The app contains no analytics, advertising, trackers, or telemetry. We do not build profiles, share data with third parties, or send unsolicited communications.

5. Your rights and control

Because the data lives only on your device, access, export (via backups), and deletion are always in your hands. To erase all data, simply clear the app's data or uninstall it, and delete any backup files you created.

6. Changes to this policy

If future versions of the app change how data is handled (for example, cloud sync), this policy will be updated before that feature reaches you, with the revision date shown at the top.
